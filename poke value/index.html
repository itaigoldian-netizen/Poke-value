Poke Value is a weighted blend of median sold prices: PSA 35%, BGS 25%, CGC 15%, EAS 15%, TAG 10%. Graders with no sales are left out and their weight is shared among the others. Chips marked "1y" used the last 365 days because that grader had fewer than 3 sales in the last 90 days.
